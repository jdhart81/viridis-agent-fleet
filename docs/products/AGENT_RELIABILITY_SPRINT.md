# Viridis Agent Reliability Sprint

**Production catalog:** live at `$995 USD`; no buyer order, offer, funding,
delivery, accepted outcome, repeat purchase, subscription, or revenue has been
verified. Buyer contact and money movement remain separately authorized.

## The outcome

Viridis takes one existing AI-agent or automation workflow and makes it
production-ready, bounded, tested, observable, and verifiably delivered.

**Introductory fixed price:** $995 USD  
**Delivery target:** five business days after complete scope and independently
verified live funding

## Who it is for

An AI automation agency, SaaS founder, or operations team that has one
repetitive workflow, access to the systems involved, a named human approver for
risky actions, explicit acceptance criteria, and a budget of at least $995.

## Included

- one existing business workflow;
- up to two existing systems or APIs;
- explicit inputs, outputs, and acceptance tests;
- deterministic execution and bounded failure behavior;
- human approval gates before payment, publication, customer messages,
  account changes, deletion, legal acceptance, or other irreversible actions;
- security and tool-policy preflight;
- automated tests, logs, and failure handling;
- deployable handoff; and
- one content-addressed delivery receipt.

## Excluded unless separately scoped

- new data licensing or rights acquisition;
- open-ended product development;
- account, identity, terms, or legal actions;
- security, legal, compliance, or financial certification;
- autonomous irreversible actions;
- indefinite maintenance or included feature work.

## Required buyer intake

1. Business outcome and current manual process.
2. Monthly run frequency and minutes per run.
3. Loaded hourly cost and monthly error/rework cost.
4. Existing systems or APIs and access posture.
5. Exact acceptance criteria.
6. Irreversible actions and the human approval owner for each.
7. Implementation budget, operating-cost ceiling, and target payback period.
8. Delivery deadline and repository-access posture.

The free Agent Value Decision uses these buyer-owned facts to return `BUY`,
`BUILD`, `DO_NOT_AUTOMATE`, or `NEEDS_INPUT`. Its value model applies a
disclosed 50% realization factor. It is a screening estimate, not a guarantee
of savings, revenue, adoption, or payback.

## Funding and delivery boundary

The existing Agent Market profile id remains `viridis-mcp-delivery`; the
profile has been repositioned rather than duplicated. It advertises only the
existing Viridis cash-escrow destination. Work cannot begin until the private
Hub independently verifies the exact buyer, seller, amount, currency, and live
custody. A listing, signature, test checkout, internal credit, or counterparty
claim is not verified funding.

Delivery evidence proves what Viridis transported and when. It does not prove
buyer acceptance, usefulness, repeat demand, or MRR. Those require separate
buyer and payment evidence.

## Optional repeat product

After an accepted Sprint, the buyer may separately choose a $149/month Viridis
Reliability Watch covering scheduled acceptance checks, schema/tool-policy and
security-regression detection, bounded alerts, and one monthly usage, failure,
and value report.

The Watch is not included, activated, subscribed, or counted as MRR by the
Sprint or the free decision. Feature work and remediation are quoted
separately.
