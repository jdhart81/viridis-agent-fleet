VIRIDIS / WU WEI ROUTER

A routing recommendation, with its assumptions visible.

The v2 router selects the lowest modeled energy among quality-eligible routes that cost no more than your baseline. If a candidate within the cost cap lacks declared power, it selects the cheapest eligible route and labels the fallback. Eligibility uses the Wilson lower bound. Execution is never authorized.

Method and limits

Modeled energy per routed decision uses caller-declared power multiplied by p95 latency and task count, with integer microjoules sealed. Modeled savings compare baseline and chosen energy. Missing declared power means insufficient data for savings; no delta is invented. A baseline-optimal decision reports zero modeled savings when power data are available.

The fee does not change the recommendation. An analysis whose modeled net savings are below the fee still delivers in full and discloses the shortfall.

ISSUED means issuer binding only; it is not evidence of payment, correctness, validation, or buyer-confirmed usefulness. Self-seals and fleet-internal use are class viridis and excluded from external adoption and revenue. Settled delivery alone does not establish VPD or MRR.
