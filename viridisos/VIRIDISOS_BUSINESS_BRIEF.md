# ViridisOS — Business Brief

**One line:** ViridisOS is the operating system for verifiable conservation — a software platform
whose every number is backed by a machine-checked theorem, sold with a certification standard no
competitor can claim.
**Date:** 2026-07-08. Derived from the research-arm product tree (`RESEARCH_PIPELINE_v2/PRODUCT_TREE.md`).

---

## The thesis

The conservation-finance market prices ecosystem services, risk, and permanence with arbitrary,
un-auditable numbers. Viridis has spent 90+ nightly research runs proving the underlying physics as
Lean-verified theorems. **ViridisOS turns that verified science into a product platform**: the theorems
are the moat, the platform is the delivery vehicle, and a certification standard is how the moat gets
sold. The research arm's product tree shows the shape isn't scattered ideas — it's one coherent system.

## The product (three parts)

**1. ViridisOS Platform** — the software system land trusts, project developers, insurers, and MRV
markets run on. Built on the existing chassis (MEASURE · VERIFY · FINANCE · DATA) plus a module runtime
where each research result plugs in as a priced module. Anchor: the platform brief (Run-021).

**2. The Certification Standard (the moat)** — a paid "certified-by-a-verified-theorem" gate on MRV,
carbon, biodiversity, and permanence claims. When a claim ships through ViridisOS, it carries a
cryptographic attestation tracing to a Lean-checked theorem + the measured data. No competitor can
issue this. Backed by the Measurement-Adequacy / Verifiability standards (Runs 046 / 050 / 044).

**3. Domain modules** — priced capabilities that plug into the platform, each backed by a specific
verified theorem. First three to ship:

| Module | What it sells | Backing theorem | Y5 ARR (est.) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mutualist** — risk-premium pricing | the first physically-grounded risk premium for natural-capital assets; the Diversification Wall | SRPT (Run-093, verified) | $18–32M |
| **Tempo** — restoration/stewardship cadence | optimal intervention timing + a certified tempo standard | Stewardship Tempo (Run-060) | $45M |
| **Restoration (Nucleation)** — planting-design law | "concentrate, don't broadcast": a go/no-go number + deposition setpoint that reframes broadcast seeding as stranded capital | FNT / Nucleation (Run-061) | $40–55M |

## Why it wins

- **The moat is invisible to competitors and legible to buyers.** A machine-checked proof is a
  correctness asset; the certification standard makes it a purchasable line item.
- **It compounds.** The research arm produces a new fundable, moated module every night, auto-harvested
  and prioritized into the product tree — the platform's roadmap writes itself.
- **It rides assets that already exist.** The conservation app, HDFM, Earth API (AlphaEarth), the DAO
  ledger, and the canon are already built; ViridisOS unifies them rather than starting over.
