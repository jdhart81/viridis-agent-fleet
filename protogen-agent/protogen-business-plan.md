## Business Model

### Revenue Streams

**1. Platform Subscriptions (40% of revenue)**
- Starter: $99/month (10 projects)
- Professional: $499/month (100 projects)
- Enterprise: $2,499/month (unlimited)
- White Label: $9,999/month

**2. Transaction Fees (45% of revenue)**
- 5% on orders <$1,000
- 4% on orders $1,000-$10,000
- 3% on orders >$10,000
- Volume discounts for enterprise

**3. Value-Added Services (15% of revenue)**
- AI design optimization: $50-500/project
- Expert consultations: $150/hour
- Expedited manufacturing: 25% premium
- Quality assurance: $100-1,000/project

- **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)**: $450
- **Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)**: $12,000
- **LTV:CAC Ratio**: 26.7:1
- **Gross Margin**: 40% (increasing to 55% at scale)
- **Payback Period**: 4 months

## Risk Analysis & Mitigation

### Technology Risks
**Risk**: CAD format compatibility issues
**Mitigation**: Partnership with major CAD vendors, open-source converters

**Risk**: AI model accuracy
**Mitigation**: Human-in-the-loop validation, continuous learning

### Market Risks
**Risk**: Slow adoption by traditional manufacturers
**Mitigation**: Focus on digital-native customers, prove ROI

**Risk**: Economic downturn affecting manufacturing
**Mitigation**: Diverse customer base, counter-cyclical pricing

### Operational Risks
**Risk**: Supplier quality control
**Mitigation**: Rigorous vetting, performance bonds, redundancy

**Risk**: Cybersecurity and IP theft
**Mitigation**: End-to-end encryption, SOC 2 compliance, insurance

### Competitive Risks
**Risk**: Large CAD companies entering market
**Mitigation**: Network effects, faster innovation, M&A readiness
